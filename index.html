The problem

Capacity scales. Operations don't. And the obvious fix can't be trusted, until now.

You can buy GPUs in an afternoon. Running them takes a team: each vendor, site and tenant adds a toolchain, a runbook, an on-call rotation, and a line in a spreadsheet after the invoice. Work waits on the two people who know the cluster, while the hardware sits idle.

You buy
You also take on
A second vendor
A second toolchain, a second runbook, a second on-call rotation. The team that knows one stack does not know the other.
A new site
Another inventory, another metrics stack, another set of credentials, and no single view of what is actually running.
More tenants
Isolation to prove, quotas to enforce, spend to attribute. Usually reconciled in a spreadsheet, after the invoice.
More GPUs
Hours nobody has. Capacity sits idle because nobody has time to place work on it.

An AI agent is the obvious answer, and the obvious risk. A generic agent holds your keys, acts on its own say-so and reports success it never checked. Nobody hands production to that.

VibOps is the agent you can hand it to.

Why you can let it act

Three guarantees, built into the platform.

An agent that can touch production has to earn it. These are not settings you turn on. They are how VibOps is built.

It never holds the keys

Your credentials stay on site.

A small gateway lives on your site and only calls out: no inbound port, no VPN. It holds the credentials. The model, whichever one you choose, never sees a secret.

It asks before it acts

Anything that can break something waits for your approval.

Before a risky action it shows you exactly what it is about to do, and stops. Your admins decide what needs an approval, and from whom.

It proves what it did

Done means read back, never assumed.

After every action it checks the real state and reports whether it is actually done. Everything lands in an audit trail that cannot be quietly edited, and your compliance reports are built from it.

How it works

You ask. It checks. You approve. It acts, and proves it.

You ask

In the console, in plain language. Or from a schedule, a pipeline, an alert, or your editor over the MCP server.

It checks

Your rules, your roles, your tenants. Anything risky comes back as a preview, not an action.

You approve

Or someone else does. The agent cannot approve on your behalf, even if the model tries.

It acts and proves it

The action runs on your site, the state is read back, the result is recorded and billed to the right team.

Getting started

Three steps. No agent on the compute machines.

VibOps sits beside your infrastructure, not in front of it.

Step 1

Deploy the control plane

A self-hosted stack, on your own ground.

$curl -fsSL https://vibops.ai/install.sh | bash
Step 2

Connect your sites

One lightweight gateway per site. It discovers the clusters, the GPUs and the hypervisors on its own, and changes nothing you have not confirmed.

Step 3

Operate

From the console, or from your editor over the MCP server. The MCP server and the Python SDK are public and MIT-licensed.

$pip install vibops-mcp
